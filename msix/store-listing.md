## Product name

EasyUniTrack

## Description

EasyUniTrack is a simple planner for uni and TAFE students. Keep your assignments, study sessions, subjects, job applications and monthly budget in one calm place, so you always know what's due and what to do next.

Everything stays on your computer. There's no account to create, nothing to sign in to, and no ads. EasyUniTrack works fully offline.

Start each day on Today: see what's overdue, what's due this week, how busy the next 7 days look, which job applications need a follow-up, and how much of your budget is left.

Add tasks with due dates, priorities, subjects and your own task types. Repeat weekly readings or tutorials automatically. Tell EasyUniTrack how many hours an assignment needs and Plan sessions spreads the work out before the due date.

Track job and internship applications on a board from Applied to Offer, with follow-up reminders. Set a monthly budget in your own currency and watch what's left as you add money in and out.

Make it yours with 6 colour themes, 5 text styles and light or dark mode. Get a daily reminder notification at the time you choose, and have EasyUniTrack open quietly when you sign in.

Your data is yours: download a backup file whenever you like, restore it in a couple of clicks, and EasyUniTrack also keeps a daily backup copy for you.

Tip: before uninstalling, use Settings > Your data > Download backup to keep a copy of your information.
